usage: close-control bas [-h] --ledger LEDGER --tax-codes TAX_CODES
                         [--payroll PAYROLL] --currency CURRENCY
                         --period-start PERIOD_START --period-end PERIOD_END
                         --basis {accruals,cash}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --ledger LEDGER       one tenant's Tenant,LineID,Date,PaidDate,AccountID,TaxC
                        ode,Gross,GST,Currency CSV of sales and purchases
  --tax-codes TAX_CODES
                        TaxCode,AccountID,Treatment CSV placing each tax code,
                        or a tax code on one account, on the BAS labels
  --payroll PAYROLL     payroll register, as `payroll` reads it, to draft W1
                        and W2
  --currency CURRENCY   three-letter currency every row carries, e.g. AUD
  --period-start PERIOD_START
                        first day of the BAS period, YYYY-MM-DD
  --period-end PERIOD_END
                        last day of the BAS period, YYYY-MM-DD
  --basis {accruals,cash}
                        the GST accounting basis: accruals places a row by the
                        earlier of Date and PaidDate, cash by PaidDate
